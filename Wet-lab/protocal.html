1.1 Forward Primer Screening

Experimental Procedures

  1. Take out the experimental reagents from the refrigerator, prepare forward primer F1 and reverse primers R1, R2, R3, thaw, oscillate and centrifuge for later use.
  2. Prepare the RNP system in accordance with the experimental system table, followed by incubation at 37℃ for 30 min.
  3. Prepare lyophilized microsphere tubes, and configure the RPA system in the tubes according to the experimental system table. Set up three groups including F1R1, F1R2 and F1R3. After oscillation, centrifugation and thorough mixing, aliquot the system into the bottom of 8-strip tubes.
  4. Upon completion of RNP system incubation, prepare the CRISPR system according to the experimental system table. Oscillate, centrifuge and mix well, then aliquot and transfer the system to the tube wall of 8-strip tubes.
  5. Add 1 μL plasmid to the cap of positive 8-strip tubes and 1 μL nuclease-free water to the cap of negative 8-strip tubes, then tightly cover the tube caps.
  6. Set the PCR instrument parameters as 40℃ and 50 cycles. Centrifuge the 8-strip tubes to fully mix all reagents, place them in the instrument tank and start the reaction.
  7. Collect and process experimental data.

1.2 Reverse Primer Screening

Experimental Procedures

2.1 Optimal Reaction Temperature

Experimental Procedures

  1. Take out the experimental reagents from the refrigerator, thaw, oscillate and centrifuge for later use.
  2. Prepare the RNP system in accordance with the experimental system table, followed by incubation at 37℃ for 30 min.
  3. Prepare lyophilized microsphere tubes, and configure the RPA system in the tubes according to the experimental system table. After oscillation, centrifugation and thorough mixing, aliquot the system into the bottom of 8-strip tubes.
  4. Upon completion of RNP system incubation, prepare the CRISPR system according to the experimental system table. Oscillate, centrifuge and mix well, then aliquot and transfer the system to the tube wall of 8-strip tubes.
  5. Add 1 μL plasmid to the cap of positive 8-strip tubes and 1 μL nuclease-free water to the cap of negative 8-strip tubes, then tightly cover the tube caps.

  7. Collect and process experimental data.
