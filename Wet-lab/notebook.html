1.1.1Respiratory Syncytial Virus RPA-CRISPR Forward Primer F1-F3 Combined Screening
2026.3.10

Prepare the experimental reagents. Remove lyophilized pellet tubes, Nuclease-free water, MgSO4, forward primer F1, F2, F3, reverse primer R2, Respiratory Syncytial Virus-specific plasmid, DEPC water, NE Buffer, LbCas12a, gRNA-RSV and Reporter from the refrigerator and thaw them. Vortex-mix all reagents thoroughly before use.

RPA section

Take three sets of 8-tube strips and label the primer groups as F1R2, F2R2, F3R2, together with positive and negative control groups. Each primer group contains one positive and one negative group, with replicate groups included for each.

Vortex and centrifuge the thawed forward primer F1, F2, F3, reverse primer R2 and Nuclease-free water.

Prepare the RPA system on a per-reaction basis. Add 5.08 μL Nuclease-free water, 0.96 μL reverse primer R2 and 0.96 μL corresponding forward primer to each tube, for a total volume of 7 μL, and place the mixture at the bottom of the tube.

Add 1 μL Respiratory Syncytial Virus-specific plasmid to each positive-reaction tube and 1 μL Nuclease-free water to each negative-reaction tube.

CRISPR section

Prepare the RNP premix by adding 2.25 μL gRNA-RSV (10 μM), 2.25 μL Cas12a (10 μM), 3.75 μL NE Buffer (10×) and 3.75 μL DEPC water, for a total volume of 12 μL. Vortex-mix and centrifuge, then incubate at 37℃ for 30 min.

Prepare the CRISPR detection system on a per-reaction basis. Add 0.8 μL incubated RNP, 0.15 μL Reporter, 3 μL MgSO4 and 3.05 μL Nuclease-free water to each tube, for a total volume of 7 μL.

Add the 7 μL CRISPR detection system to the corresponding RPA reaction tube. Together with the 7 μL RPA system and 1 μL plasmid or Nuclease-free water, the final volume is 15 μL per reaction.

Set the qPCR program with a heated-lid temperature of 105℃, a reaction volume of 15 μL, 50 cycles, a reaction temperature of 40℃, and 00:01:00 per cycle; enable fluorescence acquisition.

Place the centrifuged tubes into the qPCR instrument in order.

Turn off the ventilation, spray the workbench with 75% ethanol, turn on the UV lamp for 15 min, turn it off, wipe the workbench clean, and return all instruments and reagents to their designated locations.

After the experiment, collect the data and perform plotting and analysis. F1 was optimal.
